### Selected work

**[kkbox-retention-pricing-analysis](https://github.com/Divyansh1504/kkbox-retention-pricing-analysis)**

Retention and pricing analysis on 23 million subscription transactions. The headline number reverses under segmentation: aggregate retention appeared to be declining, but splitting by trial versus paying subscribers showed paid retention rising 7 points, with the aggregate pulled down by trial share growing from 28.5% to 38.6% of new cohorts.

Also separates free promotional grants from genuine discounts, which a naive discount scale treats as the same thing, and includes a target-leakage demonstration quantified at 0.981 versus 0.875 AUC. Ends in a recommendation memo with confidence levels stated per finding.

**[tariff-event-study](https://github.com/Divyansh1504/tariff-event-study)**

A reproducible event-study pipeline for testing whether a policy event shows up in industry and portfolio returns, applied to the 2025 US tariff announcements across six sectors.

The corrected analysis finds no significant effect in any factor specification, and a minimum-detectable-effect analysis explains why: detecting the observed alpha at 80% power would have needed roughly 443 trading days against the 126 available. That makes the honest conclusion "no detectable effect" rather than "no effect." Graduate capstone, RIT, where I owned the data pipeline and quantitative analysis.
